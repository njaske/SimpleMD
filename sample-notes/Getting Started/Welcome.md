# Welcome to SimpleMD

A calm, focused space for reading and writing Markdown. No accounts, no clutter, no noise — just your documents and a window that stays out of the way.

## Why SimpleMD

- Open an entire **folder** of notes in a single click
- Read beautifully rendered Markdown, or edit the source side by side
- Move fast with a live **outline** and full-text **search**
- Light and dark, your choice

> Good tools disappear. SimpleMD tries to.

## Finding your way

The slim **activity bar** on the far left switches between Files, Search, and Pinned. Open a few documents and they appear as tabs along the top.

When you are ready, open **Markdown Guide** to see everything SimpleMD can render — tables, code, task lists, and more.
